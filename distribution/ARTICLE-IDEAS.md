# Article ideas — living backlog

New-essay ideas only. (Repurposing the existing 57 articles lives in
`back-catalog-queue.md`; distribution lessons live in `LEARNING-LOG.md`.)

**How to use:** add ideas at the bottom of the relevant section as they occur —
a title, one line on the angle, and the axes. Promote an idea by moving its
status forward: `idea → outlined → drafting → published (date, slug)`. Prune
ruthlessly; a backlog only works if dead ideas get deleted. Review when picking
the next essay to write (roughly every 2 weeks, per the release cadence).

Axes (same as `back-catalog-queue.md`): **B** breakout (travels beyond
finance) · **E** evergreen · **N** news-peggable · **L** list/framework format.
