# LinkedIn newsletter — editions 4–8 (frames)

Editions 1–3 are fully drafted as standalone files. For 4–8 here's the efficient
form: each edition's *body* is the essay itself (already written, in
`src/content/insights/`), so this gives you only the **frame** — the title, the
one-line kicker to open with, the close/CTA, and the canonical link. Paste the
essay body between the kicker and the close, lightly adapted for LinkedIn's
editor. Links here are safe (the newsletter is exempt from the feed link penalty).

Cadence: one edition every ~2 weeks, ~1 week after each essay's site + Substack
versions. See `launch-checklist.md` for the running order.
